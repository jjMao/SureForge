# Code of conduct

This is a small project about careful work, and the same standard applies to how people treat each other here.

Be direct about the work and courteous to the person. Criticize text, evidence, and decisions; do not attack contributors. Assume a report is made in good faith until shown otherwise, and say so plainly when you were wrong.

Do not post other people's personal information, private conversations, or transcripts, including material an agent produced from someone's private context. Do not use the repository to harass anyone, on any basis.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that breaks these rules and may block accounts that keep doing so. To raise a concern about conduct, use GitHub's report feature on the content in question or open an issue marked "conduct".
